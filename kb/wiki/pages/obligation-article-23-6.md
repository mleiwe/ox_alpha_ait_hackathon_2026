# obligation-article-23-6

**6 Importers shall provide the relevant competent authorities, upon a reasoned re**

6 Importers shall provide the relevant competent authorities, upon a reasoned request, with all the necessary information and documentation, including that referred to in paragraph 5, to demonstrate the conformity of a high-risk AI system with the requirements set out in Section 2 in a language which can be easily understood by them.
