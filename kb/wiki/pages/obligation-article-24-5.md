# obligation-article-24-5

**Where the high-risk AI system presents a risk within the meaning of Article 79(1**

Where the high-risk AI system presents a risk within the meaning of Article 79(1), the distributor shall immediately inform the provider or importer of the system and the authorities competent for the high-risk AI system concerned, giving details, in particular, of the non-compliance and of any corrective actions taken.
