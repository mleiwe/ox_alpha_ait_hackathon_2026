# obligation-article-40-0

**Article 40 — Harmonised standards and standardisation deliverables ## 1 High-ris**

Article 40 — Harmonised standards and standardisation deliverables ## 1 High-risk AI systems or general-purpose AI models which are in conformity with harmonised standards or parts thereof the references of which have been published in the in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 shall be presumed to be in conformity with the requirements set out in Section 2 of this Chapter or, as applicable, with the obligations set out in of Chapter V, Sections 2 and 3, of this Regulation, to the extent that those standards cover those requirements or obligations.
