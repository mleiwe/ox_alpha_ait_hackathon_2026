# obligation-article-25-3

**In particular, the obligation laid down in the second subparagraph shall include**

In particular, the obligation laid down in the second subparagraph shall include, where relevant for the purposes specified therein, the following: ## (a) making available of technical documentation sufficient to assess compliance with the requirements laid down in Article 16; ## (b) informing the new providers about known limitations and failure modes; and ## (c) providing the new providers with targeted technical access, including for testing and validation.
