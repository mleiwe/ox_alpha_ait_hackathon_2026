# obligation-article-49-0

**Article 49 — Registration ## 1 Before placing on the market or putting into serv**

Article 49 — Registration ## 1 Before placing on the market or putting into service a high-risk AI system listed in Annex III, with the exception of high-risk AI systems referred to in point 2 of Annex III, the provider or, where applicable, the authorised representative shall register themselves and their system in the EU database referred to in Article 71.
