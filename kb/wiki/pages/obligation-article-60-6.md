# obligation-article-60-6

**6 In accordance with Article 75, Member States shall confer on their market surv**

6 In accordance with Article 75, Member States shall confer on their market surveillance authorities the powers of requiring providers and prospective providers to provide information, of carrying out unannounced remote or on-site inspections, and of performing checks on the conduct of the testing in real world conditions and the related high-risk AI systems.
