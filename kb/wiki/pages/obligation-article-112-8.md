# obligation-article-112-8

**5 By 2 August 2028, the Commission shall evaluate the functioning of the AI Offi**

5 By 2 August 2028, the Commission shall evaluate the functioning of the AI Office, whether the AI Office has been given sufficient powers and competences to fulfil its tasks, and whether it would be relevant and needed for the proper implementation and enforcement of this Regulation to upgrade the AI Office and its enforcement competences and to increase its resources.
