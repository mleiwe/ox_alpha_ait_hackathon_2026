# obligation-article-112-10

**6 By 2 August 2028 and every four years thereafter, the Commission shall submit **

6 By 2 August 2028 and every four years thereafter, the Commission shall submit a report on the review of the progress on the development of standardisation deliverables on the energy-efficient development of general-purpose AI models, and asses the need for further measures or actions, including binding measures or actions.
