# obligation-article-74-5

**4 The procedures referred to in Articles 79 to 83 of this Regulation shall not a**

4 The procedures referred to in Articles 79 to 83 of this Regulation shall not apply to AI systems related to products covered by the Union harmonisation legislation listed in section A of Annex I, where such legal acts already provide for procedures ensuring an equivalent level of protection and having the same objective.
