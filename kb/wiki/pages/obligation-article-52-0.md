# obligation-article-52-0

**Article 52 — Procedure ## 1 Where a general-purpose AI model meets the condition**

Article 52 — Procedure ## 1 Where a general-purpose AI model meets the condition referred to in Article 51(1), point (a), the relevant provider shall notify the Commission without delay and in any event within two weeks after that requirement is met or it becomes known that it will be met.
