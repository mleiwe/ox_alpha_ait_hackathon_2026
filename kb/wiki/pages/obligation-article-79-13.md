# obligation-article-79-13

In particular, the market surveillance authorities shall indicate whether the non-compliance is due to one or more of the following: ### (a) non-compliance with the prohibition of the AI practices referred to in Article 5; ### (b) a failure of a high-risk AI system to meet requirements set out in Chapter III, Section 2; ### (c) shortcomings in the harmonised standards or common specifications referred to in Articles 40 and 41 conferring a presumption of conformity; ### (d) non-compliance with Article 50.
