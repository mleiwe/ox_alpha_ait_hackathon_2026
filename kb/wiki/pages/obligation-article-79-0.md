# obligation-article-79-0

**Article 79 — Procedure at national level for dealing with AI systems presenting **

Article 79 — Procedure at national level for dealing with AI systems presenting a risk ## 1 AI systems presenting a risk shall be understood as a ‘product presenting a risk’ as defined in Article 3, point 19 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, in so far as they present risks to the health or safety, or to fundamental rights, of persons.
