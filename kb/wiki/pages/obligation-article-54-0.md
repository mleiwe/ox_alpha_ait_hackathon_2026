# obligation-article-54-0

**Article 54 — Authorised representatives of providers of general-purpose AI model**

Article 54 — Authorised representatives of providers of general-purpose AI models ## 1 Prior to placing a general-purpose AI model on the Union market, providers established in third countries shall, by written mandate, appoint an authorised representative which is established in the Union.
