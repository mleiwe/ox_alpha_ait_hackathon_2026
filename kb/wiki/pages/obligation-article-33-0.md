# obligation-article-33-0

**Article 33 — Subsidiaries of notified bodies and subcontracting ## 1 Where a not**

Article 33 — Subsidiaries of notified bodies and subcontracting ## 1 Where a notified body subcontracts specific tasks connected with the conformity assessment or has recourse to a subsidiary, it shall ensure that the subcontractor or the subsidiary meets the requirements laid down in Article 31, and shall inform the notifying authority accordingly.
