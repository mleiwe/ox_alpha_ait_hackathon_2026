# obligation-article-23-1

**2 Where an importer has sufficient reason to consider that a high-risk AI system**

2 Where an importer has sufficient reason to consider that a high-risk AI system is not in conformity with this Regulation, or is falsified, or accompanied by falsified documentation, it shall not place the system on the market until it has been brought into conformity.
