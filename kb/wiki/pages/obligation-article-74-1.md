# obligation-article-74-1

**For the purposes of the effective enforcement of this Regulation: ### (a) any re**

For the purposes of the effective enforcement of this Regulation: ### (a) any reference to an economic operator under Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 shall be understood as including all operators identified in Article 2(1) of this Regulation; ### (b) any reference to a product under Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 shall be understood as including all AI systems falling within the scope of this Regulation.
