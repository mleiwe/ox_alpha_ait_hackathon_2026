# obligation-article-37-0

**Article 37 — Challenge to the competence of notified bodies ## 1 The Commission **

Article 37 — Challenge to the competence of notified bodies ## 1 The Commission shall, where necessary, investigate all cases where there are reasons to doubt the competence of a notified body or the continued fulfilment by a notified body of the requirements laid down in Article 31 and of its applicable responsibilities.
